---

Opisometer measures distance and area on Apple Maps, for iPhone and iPad. Drop points, follow roads and trails or go as the crow flies, close a loop for the area inside, record a route as you move, and see every climb and descent. No account, no ads, no tracking.

This repository is the support channel for the app. To report a problem or request a feature, [open an issue](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/opisometer/issues/new) with your device, its iOS version, and what you expected to see. The [support page](https://chrismahlke.github.io/opisometer/support/) answers common questions.
