<h1 align="center">Opisometer Support</h1>

<p align="center"><sub>HELP, BUG REPORTS AND FEATURE REQUESTS<br>FOR THE OPISOMETER APP ON IPHONE AND IPAD</sub></p>

Opisometer measures distance and area on Apple Maps. Touch and hold to drop points, follow roads, trails and bike routes or go straight as the crow flies, close a loop for the area inside, record a route as you move, and see every climb and descent. This repository is the public home for support. The app's source code is kept in a private repository.

## Get help

- **Report a bug or request a feature:** [open an issue](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/opisometer-support/issues/new). Search the [existing issues](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/opisometer-support/issues) first in case it has already been raised.
- **Email:** [cmahlke@gmail.com](mailto:cmahlke@gmail.com?subject=Opisometer%20support) for anything you would rather not post publicly.
- **Support page:** [chrismahlke.github.io/opisometer/support](https://chrismahlke.github.io/opisometer/support/) answers the most common questions.

## Writing a useful bug report

Include as much of the following as you can:

- Your device and its iOS or iPadOS version
- The version of Opisometer, shown in Settings
- What you were doing, step by step
- What you expected to happen and what happened instead
- Roughly where on the map you were measuring, if the problem concerns routes, elevation or map imagery
- A screenshot or screen recording, if it helps show the problem

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26.5 or later
- An internet connection for maps, search, routes and elevation
- Location access while using the app, to see where you are or record a route (optional)
